01Dex

Every legal Pokémon, at a glance.

The Dex shows only what's legal in your team's regulation, with how often each one is used, so you're never building around something you can't bring.

02Team

Build without leaving the Dex.

Open any Pokémon to check its stats and abilities, then add it to your team in one tap. Your six stay in view as you go.
