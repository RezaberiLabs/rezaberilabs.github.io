Poke Lab Support

A team builder for VGC doubles, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Get help

Email support@rezaberi.com with your device, the app version (Settings › About) and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Common questions

Why can't I find a Pokémon or move? Poke Lab only lists what is legal in the team's format. Change the format from the pill under the team name.

Where are my teams saved? On your device, and in your iCloud account if sync is on.

Does the app work offline? Yes, after the first launch.
