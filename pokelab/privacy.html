Poke Lab Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026 · Rezaberi Labs

Poke Lab does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Your teams

Teams you build are saved on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are stored in your own iCloud account by Apple. We cannot see them.

Game data

The app downloads public game-rules data (which Pokémon, moves and items are legal in each format) from our server. These downloads send nothing about you or your teams. Like any website, our hosting provider may briefly keep standard request logs (such as IP address) for security and reliability; we do not use them to identify or track you.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple collects these, not Poke Lab, and they do not identify you. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Poke Lab is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
